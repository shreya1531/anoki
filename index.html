THE ANOKHI EDIT

A little more clarity.

Are natural and grown diamonds real diamonds?+

Yes. Both share the defining physical, chemical and optical properties of diamond. Their origins differ, and individual grading and certification remain important.

Can I request a personalised design?+

Yes. Start with the guided form above. Share your category, occasion, style, diamond preference, approximate budget and timeline, and the atelier can help shape a design direction around your story.

What happens after I approve a design?+

Anokhi shares the CAD or design proof for your approval and confirms approval by email. Your piece is then crafted for you, typically in 15 days. You receive a ready-to-dispatch update and a secure balance-payment link before delivery.

How are specifications and price confirmed?+

Ask for a current quote before you decide. It should confirm the product specifications, key price components, any applicable discount and tax, and the final amount. The website's design imagery is illustrative.

What are Anokhi's buyback and exchange policies?+

Anokhi offers lifetime buyback at 80% value and 100% value on exchange of diamonds, irrespective of diamond type, as per brand policy. Ask the atelier to confirm the terms for your piece.
